## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Developer communities, creative tooling, incident education, and energetic product launches. Avoid when: Quiet financial reporting or neutral enterprise documentation must dominate.

### Signature Atoms

- Aubergine rgb(31, 22, 51), white rgb(255, 255, 255), pink rgb(253, 68, 176), violet rgb(106, 95, 193).
- Rubik with Noto Sans SC; adapted hero 76px, weight 800, line-height 1.17, tracking -.035em.
- Centered 830px minimum hero with a 7px offset text shadow and a low 220px diagnostic frame rotated -2deg.
- Buttons have 8px corners and 2px accent outlines; primary actions use white fill with aubergine labels.
- Paired capability cards have 8px corners, 2px borders, 24px gaps, and 5px violet offset edges.
- 62px pink metric numerals; chart has a 2px violet border and 13px pink signal tracks.

### Do

- Use open-source Rubik and Noto Sans SC; treat Dammit Sans only as a visual reference.
- Center oversized white headlines above a wide, low diagnostic abstraction.
- Keep explanatory copy white and use hot pink for metrics, state signals, and outlined accents.
- Use solid violet offset edges and lightly tilted diagnostic regions instead of soft dashboard elevation.
- Keep signal-bar geometry decorative and non-interactive; preserve real controls as semantic elements.

### Don't

- Do not reduce the palette to generic black with a single green database accent.
- Do not shrink the display into subdued corporate typography.
- Do not replace thick outlines and offset edges with uniformly soft shadows.
- Do not fill decorative diagnostics with copied traces, code, or fabricated incident claims.
- Do not copy Sentry logos, trademarks, screenshots, illustrations, or marketing copy.
