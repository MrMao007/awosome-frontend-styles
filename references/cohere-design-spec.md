## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Enterprise platforms, research services, organizational launches and nature-inflected technology stories. Avoid when: Compact transactional screens or colorful playful campaigns must lead with density.

### Signature Atoms

- White canvas rgb(255,255,255), ink rgb(33,33,33), dark actions rgb(23,23,28), pale panels rgb(242,242,242).
- Space Grotesk hero 83px, weight 400, line-height 1.17 and tracking -.035em; copy width up to 1150px.
- Landscape stage has 40px side gutters and 12px corners, with an 8px inner abstract software pane.
- Original contour rings repeat #657747, #b6b274, #d8c39a and #9d925e under a dark diagonal overlay.
- Dark action and filter pills use 999px radius; hero actions have 38px minimum height and 13px labels.
- Three-column pale library uses 22px gaps, 12px white-panel corners and 25px regular-weight titles.

### Do

- Use Space Grotesk with Noto Sans SC; treat CohereText and Unica77 Cohere Web as source references only.
- Center a very large regular-weight statement above a wide landscape-oriented preview stage.
- Combine original organic contour texture with disciplined blank software-pane framing.
- Keep hero actions discreet and dark, leaving typography and landscape texture to carry the identity.
- Reset supporting content to pale neutral library panels with generous spacing and restrained metadata.

### Don't

- Do not copy Cohere logos, trademarks, product screenshots, contour artwork, illustrations or marketing copy.
- Do not shrink the opening into a utility prompt or make the display type heavy and condensed.
- Do not add colorful CTA clusters, neon glow or a dense first-view dashboard.
- Do not extend the organic texture into body text backgrounds or use it as a logo substitute.
- Do not inflate card shadows or turn every light content section into a dark cinematic field.
