## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Developer platforms, infrastructure tools, technical services and production-oriented product launches. Avoid when: Warm editorial storytelling or colorful consumer campaigns require expressive type and imagery.

### Signature Atoms

- Canvas rgb(4,8,4), ink rgb(227,229,225), green accent rgb(127,238,100), panels rgb(16,18,15).
- Inter hero 45px, weight 400, line-height 1.2 and tracking -.025em; supporting copy is 16px.
- Pale primary pills use 48px corners, 38px minimum height and dark panel-colored labels.
- Original crossed particle field repeats on a 10px grid with #7fee64 dots and #a8c895 secondary dots.
- Technical panels use 7px corners and 1px #4b4d4a66 rules; three-column inventory uses 20px gaps.
- Feature headings are 23px regular weight; #a8aba6 descriptions and green monospace metadata sit on #10120f.

### Do

- Use Inter with Noto Sans SC at regular weight; treat ABC Diatype as an observed source reference only.
- Keep the centered introduction calm and mid-sized, leaving visual density to a broad original particle field.
- Use black-green surfaces, pale primary action pills and green selected states rather than green-filled controls everywhere.
- Keep feature inventory sober with small-radius dark panels, thin separators and monospace metadata.
- Keep particle decoration noninteractive and separate from readable text, with restrained glow confined to the graphic.

### Don't

- Do not copy Modal logos, trademarks, product screenshots, particle structures, source code or marketing copy.
- Do not turn the original crossed dotted field into the brand's logo or replicate its proprietary composition.
- Do not use oversized bold sales typography or a white pastel enterprise canvas.
- Do not flood every control and panel with green, neon outlines or exaggerated glow.
- Do not replace the compact rounded technical inventory with oversized glass cards and deep shadows.
