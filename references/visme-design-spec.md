## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Resource libraries, onboarding pages, learning portals, and approachable professional services. Avoid when: A monumental campaign headline or gritty technical console is essential.

### Signature Atoms

- Canvas #ffffff, ink #293745, action #3d92e7, soft surface #f8fafb, rules #dae4ea.
- Lato with Noto Sans SC for the adapted hero at 40px, weight 600, line-height 1.3, tracking -.015em.
- Montserrat with Noto Sans SC for section headings at 43px, weight 600, line-height 1.27.
- White creation tray width 860px, min-height 152px, radius 16px, and 0 10px 30px #29374518 shadow.
- Hero and filter capsules use 999px corners; general action token is 6px; template tiles have 16px corners.
- Template tiles use thin #dae4ea outlines and preview aspect-ratio 2.7; final blue CTA labels use #172a35.

### Do

- Use Lato and Noto Sans SC for a moderate hero greeting, reserving Montserrat for larger section headings.
- Keep the opening canvas white with restrained blue-gray haze and a quiet header.
- Place real actions at the bottom of a wide raised white tray rather than adding an unsupported generation input.
- Use compact blue controls, pale outlined secondary actions, and orderly template-choice tiles downstream.
- Retain #172a35 labels on the final blue CTA and verify contrast for blue labels and selected controls on light surfaces.
- Preserve readable copy and usable tray actions on mobile instead of enlarging the opening into a poster.

### Don't

- Do not copy Visme logos, trademarks, templates, customer marks, product screenshots, or marketing copy.
- Do not substitute an enormous display slogan for the moderate first-viewport greeting.
- Do not turn the soft atmospheric haze into saturated rainbow glows or a dark theater.
- Do not remove the delivered dark final-CTA label correction or assume pale text works on every blue control.
- Do not make every content surface heavily shadowed or invent a functional AI prompt field.
