## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Workshop pages, visual learning tools, participatory community experiences. Avoid when: Formal compliance messaging or tightly packed data grids dominate.

### Signature Atoms

- #f0f0f0 canvas, #1c1c1e ink, #3859ff actions, and white #fff boards
- Manrope with Noto Sans SC; hero 56px, weight 500, line-height 1.16, tracking -.025em
- Hero dot grid repeats every 20px; lower hero corners use 40px radii
- 6px-radius blue buttons, 170px minimum width and 48px minimum height
- Floating 20px-radius board with yellow #ffdc4a22 and pink #f17de522 glows; curved #6161ff connector
- Sticky-note cards use 2px corners, #fff8bc, #e1d5ff, or #ddf4dd, and slight opposing rotations

### Do

- Use Manrope display type and Inter body copy, with Noto Sans SC for Chinese.
- Center medium-weight copy on a quiet dotted gray canvas and keep actions rectangular blue.
- Connect original floating board abstractions with thin curved paths around, not through, the copy.
- Use pastel sticky-note surfaces and slight rotations to add workshop energy below the hero.
- Limit yellow and pink glow to the central collage; keep metric rows neutral and open.

### Don't

- Do not copy Miro logos, trademarks, collaboration boards, illustrations, or marketing copy.
- Do not replace the light canvas with a dark neon workspace.
- Do not make the dot grid dark enough to compete with text.
- Do not round sticky notes into pill-like cards or remove their tactile rotation.
- Do not turn curved connectors into thick ornamental borders around every section.
