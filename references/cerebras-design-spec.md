## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Industrial launches, infrastructure platforms, hardware showcases and cinematic engineering presentations. Avoid when: Friendly consumer onboarding or long-form editorial reading needs soft approachable framing.

### Signature Atoms

- White canvas rgb(255,255,255), ink rgb(26,26,26), orange accent rgb(241,90,39), warm surface rgb(236,232,228).
- Navigation is 108px high; the dark hero has 825px minimum height and a 32px rgb(255,152,92) top edge.
- Adapted Manrope hero is 49px, weight 400, line-height 1.25 and tracking -.04em; copy anchors low-left.
- Original #050409 corridor uses clipped diagonal rack panels with repeated #151515 and #060606 lines.
- Actions have 2px corners and 12px monospace labels; hardware feature panels have 0px corners and joined 1px #ccc9c7 rules.
- Dark instrumentation strip uses #1a1a1a backing, 57px white metric figures and #ffffff2c dividers.

### Do

- Use open-source Manrope with Noto Sans SC at regular weight and monospace labels for practical controls.
- Give an original dark vanishing-point scene most of the first-view area beneath a tall white navigation shell.
- Place required new-content copy low-left; treat its hero scale as an adaptation rather than an observed source headline.
- Use black text on orange actions and keep supporting features hard-edged, joined and thinly ruled.
- Reset below the scene with a restrained dark instrumentation strip, pale hardware panels and an orange closing field.

### Don't

- Do not copy Cerebras logos, trademarks, product screenshots, server photography, illustrations or marketing copy.
- Do not claim the source first view contained the adapted headline or invent unsupported performance claims.
- Do not substitute a glowing software dashboard for the large corridor-oriented media composition.
- Do not soften every component into pills, pastel cards or deep floating shadows.
- Do not reproduce or simulate media playback controls on an abstract decorative scene.
