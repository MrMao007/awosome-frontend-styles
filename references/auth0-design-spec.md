## Adaptation to the NOVA Example

The Aeonik Latin face is legally approximated by Manrope; Chinese uses Noto Sans SC. NOVA has no added identity form or tracking. The genuine existing modal handles both actions and the weekly chart still displays only NOVA sample values.

The observed source display family is Aeonik, sans-serif. The configured display stack is 'Manrope','Noto Sans SC',sans-serif; the UI font reference is Manrope from its open-source Google Fonts release as a legal visual substitute, not the original proprietary face. ## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Enterprise campaigns, membership platforms, service launches, and trust-focused product introductions. Avoid when: Light editorial minimalism or dense operational dashboards are required.

### Signature Atoms

- Plum rgb(18, 0, 33), off-white rgb(255, 254, 250), coral rgb(221, 85, 112), purple rgb(103, 6, 171).
- Atmospheric base rgb(24, 3, 99); coral and purple are sampled image colors used by the adaptation.
- Manrope with Noto Sans SC; adapted hero 62px, weight 400, line-height 1.2, tracking -.035em.
- 82px deep-plum navigation; centered hero with 780px minimum height and restrained white type.
- Conversion strip is capped at 600px, with 4px corners, 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5) outline, translucent fill.
- Transparent three-column cards and filters have 4px corners; chart tracks are 14px high with white fills.

### Do

- Use open-source Manrope and Noto Sans SC; treat observed Aeonik only as a visual reference.
- Center a calm, regular-weight headline in one broad coral, violet, and indigo atmospheric stage.
- Group genuine conversion actions into a translucent rectangular strip rather than separate floating pills.
- Carry thin white outlines and small corners through filters, transparent cards, and charts.
- Treat sampled hero colors as visual adaptation inputs, not verified official brand tokens.

### Don't

- Do not add work-email collection or imitation identity forms solely to mimic the source.
- Do not apply separate luminous gradients to every feature card.
- Do not replace rectangular strips with oversized pill controls and inflated corner radii.
- Do not use extra-heavy typography or dense decorative product mockups in the atmospheric hero.
- Do not copy Auth0 logos, trademarks, screenshots, illustrations, or marketing copy.
