## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Enterprise launches, professional education, workflow platforms and proof-led service pages. Avoid when: Intimate editorial reading or rugged industrial styling needs understated typography.

### Signature Atoms

- White canvas rgb(255,255,255), navy ink rgb(9,11,34), pale showroom rgb(247,247,247).
- Inter hero 69px, weight 500, line-height 1.12 and tracking -.065em; section titles 40px.
- Centered title occupies a 970px maximum width above a wide showcase with 112px desktop side gutters.
- Proof pill uses 999px radius; paired action rectangles use 10px corners and 48px minimum height.
- Showcase and white feature panels use 16px corners; segmented filter backing is #e9e9eb.
- Three-column feature grid uses 24px gaps; panel titles are 25px and supporting text is 14px.

### Do

- Use Inter with Noto Sans SC; treat Basiersquare Webfont as an observed reference, not a font asset to copy.
- Center a tightly tracked large navy statement with a small proof pill and paired actions directly underneath.
- Keep the primary canvas white and restrict navy to typography, controls and occasional contrast sections.
- Place a broad pale tabbed showroom below the headline and use original geometric preview stages.
- Present supporting content in spacious white panels, preserving clear tab states and readable dark text.

### Don't

- Do not copy Synthesia logos, trademarks, product screenshots, avatar thumbnails, illustrations or marketing copy.
- Do not wash the whole canvas blue or replace the neutral showroom with colorful glow.
- Do not reduce the opening to a small left utility heading or remove its centered proof hierarchy.
- Do not use square brutalist panels, oversized pills for every card or deep floating shadows.
- Do not decorate preview panels with invented customer faces, testimonials or unsupported enterprise claims.
