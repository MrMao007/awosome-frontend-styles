## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Product catalogs, template libraries, curated collections, and commercial portfolio pages. Avoid when: Immersive storytelling or freeform collage should outweigh orderly browsing.

### Signature Atoms

- Canvas and cards #ffffff, ink and action #080b11, supporting copy #676f7e, framing #eeeeef.
- Inter with Noto Sans SC; adapted heading 53px, weight 600, line-height 1.18, tracking -.045em.
- Observed tall preview frames are approximately 475px high, with 12px corners and 24px gaps in a four-slot strip.
- Compact dark actions use 8px corners, 42px hero min-height, 14px labels, and white text.
- Catalog tiles use 12px corners, 370px min-height, 30px padding, and thin pale framing.
- Anonymous graphic preview inserts are 90px tall with 6px corners; restrained chart tracks are 6px tall.

### Do

- Use Inter and Noto Sans SC with economical semibold display text and subdued captions.
- Center the headline above compact dark actions and an orderly strip of tall preview frames.
- Preserve four-slot framing and spacing; use original neutral plates rather than inventing observed loaded imagery.
- Use tall catalog tiles with thin pale borders, small labels, and restrained black geometric inserts.
- Keep metrics and charts monochrome and quiet so the catalog remains the page's main visual structure.
- Reduce the catalog columns responsively without overlapping fixed-width preview decorations.

### Don't

- Do not copy Kittl logos, trademarks, templates, customer artwork, product screenshots, or marketing copy.
- Do not claim generated preview artwork was visible in the source's unloaded media slots.
- Do not replace the catalog strip with irregular floating collage planes or a dark cinematic stage.
- Do not add bright action colors, diffuse glows, or large pill corners across the interface.
- Do not inflate the restrained headline into an oversized extra-heavy campaign poster.
