## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Performance tools, specialist utilities, engineering comparisons, and concise technical launches. Avoid when: Soft rounded consumer styling or an illustration-led brand is required.

### Signature Atoms

- White rgb(255, 255, 255), surface rgb(246, 246, 246), ink rgb(10, 10, 10), pink rgb(255, 31, 143).
- Archivo with Noto Sans SC; adapted display 60px, weight 800, line-height 1.08, tracking -.05em.
- Split hero uses a 515px text column and a square benchmark frame capped at 557px wide and 345px high.
- Content and controls have 0px corners; black action strip has a clipped 10px top corner and 48px minimum controls.
- Two-column zero-gap capability matrix has 1px black rules; metric numerals use ui-monospace at 43px.
- Actual chart tracks are 24px high with black fills and 4px pink terminal borders; header has a 20px black top edge.

### Do

- Use open-source Archivo and Noto Sans SC with heavy display and monospaced metrics.
- Balance the split hero with an empty square benchmark abstraction and segmented top controls.
- Group real actions into a clipped black strip, keeping pink as one precise emphasis.
- Use shared-edge black ruled feature cells and square chart bars with pink end markers.
- Keep Chinese glyph proportions natural instead of horizontally squashing them to imitate condensed Latin type.

### Don't

- Do not make the page cute, mascot-led, or pastel-colored.
- Do not introduce rounded floating cards, pill buttons, or soft glowing backgrounds.
- Do not fabricate runtime labels, timings, comparisons, or installation scripts as decorative content.
- Do not spread hot pink over large content surfaces or replace the black benchmark bars.
- Do not copy Bun logos, trademarks, screenshots, illustrations, or marketing copy.
