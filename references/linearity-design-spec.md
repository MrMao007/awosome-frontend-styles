## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Premium creative launches, compact campaign pages, and polished product demonstrations. Avoid when: Warm long-form editorial reading or dense operational interfaces are primary.

### Signature Atoms

- Canvas #050505, text #ffffff, orange #ff5e23, subdued surface rgba(255,255,255,.1).
- DM Sans with Noto Sans SC; adapted promise 49px, weight 700, line-height 1.2, tracking -.03em; Inter body.
- Role capsule uses 999px corners, 17px labels, and an inset white highlight above centered copy.
- Beveled action tray width 550px, min-height 150px, radius 26px, with inset top and bottom lighting.
- Jewel actions have 999px corners and 4px #050505 rims; orange gradient runs #ff5e23 to #ff4800.
- Feature panels use 26px corners and 1px translucent outlines; path nodes use 55px circles or rotated 8px-corner squares.

### Do

- Use DM Sans and Noto Sans SC for a modest centered promise, with Inter for body and tool labels.
- Keep the surrounding canvas almost empty and near-black; introduce the headline with a glossy role capsule.
- Group actual actions in a beveled charcoal tray with inset lighting and jewel-like orange conversion controls.
- Distinguish orange gradient layers from the restrained white interface instead of spreading neon across the page.
- Use fine original vector paths at the edges, small tool labels, and subtle inset highlights on feature tiles.
- Keep metrics as a quiet lower ribbon and simplify decorative paths and tray spacing on mobile.

### Don't

- Do not copy Linearity logos, trademarks, campaign assets, product screenshots, or marketing copy.
- Do not replace the modest promise with a giant poster or fill the empty canvas with illustration.
- Do not flatten beveled controls into generic square buttons or replace charcoal with light paper surfaces.
- Do not reduce layered orange and yellow highlights to an invented universal neon palette.
- Do not fabricate a working campaign-generation input inside the decorative action tray.
