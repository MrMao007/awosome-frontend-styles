## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Creative portfolios, learning spaces, reflective lifestyle product launches. Avoid when: Dense transactional workflows or severe technical austerity is required.

### Signature Atoms

- Warm #faf8f5 canvas, #030302 ink and actions, #dededb document rules
- Lora with Noto Serif SC; hero 63px, weight 400, line-height 1.25, tracking -.045em
- Hero inset 20px with 24px corners and sky gradient #95d6ed to #c4e6f1
- Floating #ffffffb8 navigation: 100px radius, 54px minimum height, 14px backdrop blur
- Torn-paper mask combines #a7baf2, #fafafa, and #f9e586 above an 850px-wide document abstraction
- 20px-radius library tiles with 25px gaps and #fff0f5, #ffffe9, or #eff8ed accents

### Do

- Use Lora and Noto Serif SC for regular serif headings, with Inter and Noto Sans SC body text.
- Center the hero copy inside an inset sky scene with generous negative space.
- Float translucent pill navigation above the scene and keep primary actions black.
- Create an original torn-paper geometric collage and let a document abstraction rise into the lower fold.
- Use softly framed pastel library tiles with restrained shadows and regular serif titles.

### Don't

- Do not copy Craft logos, trademarks, product screenshots, illustrations, or marketing copy.
- Do not replace the serif voice with heavy geometric sans-serif display text.
- Do not use a full-bleed hard-edged hero or opaque rectangular navigation.
- Do not turn the sky scene into a dark neon dashboard.
- Do not treat every collage color as a global action or status color.
