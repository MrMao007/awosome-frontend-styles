# Style Selection Matrix — 110 Presets

Choose one primary style from the user's subject, audience, content density, and desired emotional tone. If the user names a style, do not override it.

## Ten Foundational Systems

| Style | Best for | Character | Avoid when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creative Mode | Creative portfolios, motion/editorial showcases, expressive product stories | Cream canvas, saturated candy accents, uppercase Archivo Black, hard shadows | The page must feel quiet, conservative, or text-heavy |
| BlockFrame | Youth brands, campaigns, maximalist portfolios, energetic landing pages | Thick black borders, five candy pastels, square cards, tilted decoration | The audience expects restraint or premium minimalism |
| Biennale Yellow | Exhibitions, cultural programs, essays, literary editorial | Parchment, indigo ink, solar blooms, Instrument Serif, 1px hairlines | Dense operational dashboards or playful consumer UI |
| Blue Professional | Consulting, enterprise, finance, strategy, executive dashboards | Warm cream, single cobalt accent, soft tinted cards, Space Grotesk + Inter | The brief asks for loud, handmade, or rebellious expression |
| Bold Poster | Campaigns, announcements, launches, magazine covers | Shrikhand display, red accent, heavy rules, tilted poster energy | Long-form reading or conservative corporate communication |
| Broadside | Manifestos, advocacy, industrial/editorial stories, bold data statements | Ink/orange registers, massive lowercase Barlow, mono chrome, protest-poster tone | Soft lifestyle, luxury, or friendly rounded UI |
| Capsule | Lifestyle, beauty, fashion, wellness, playful consumer products | Rounded pills, candy fills, Bodoni Moda, gentle hard-offset lift | Dense technical content or strict institutional communication |
| Cartesian | Architecture, museums, premium editorial, research narratives | Sandstone, black/taupe, Playfair Display, compass rings, extreme restraint | High-energy campaigns or color-rich consumer experiences |
| Cobalt Grid | Technical reports, archives, datasets, catalogues, research systems | Cream/cobalt two-color grid, Newsreader, DM Mono, pixel-stack data | Warm lifestyle storytelling or rounded playful interfaces |
| Coral | Product launches, magazine stories, portfolios, event campaigns | Coral/ink/cream surfaces, Bebas Neue, hard region splits, hatch texture | Quiet luxury, dense long-form text, or rounded UI |

## Product-Inspired Presets

The following 100 presets are independent studies of product homepages, not official brand systems. Categories describe the reference product, not a restriction on your output. Select by visual character and audience. Display names and IDs both work; “Modal” resolves to `modalai`, “monday.com” to `monday`, “Together AI” to `together`, and “Devin Desktop” to `devin`.

## Recommendation Procedure

Match the brief's audience, content density, light/dark preference, typography and mood against this matrix. Shortlist at most three; reject entries matching “Avoid when”; read one chosen specification. Do not choose by brand fame or industry alone. Explicit user selection takes precedence. Machine-readable metadata and paths are in [styles.json](../styles.json); do not load all 110 specifications at once.

## Tie-breakers

- Blue Professional vs Cobalt Grid: choose Blue Professional for executive persuasion; choose Cobalt Grid for technical or archival density.
- Bold Poster vs Coral: choose Bold Poster for typographic poster drama; choose Coral for modular multi-surface storytelling.
- Creative Mode vs BlockFrame: choose Creative Mode for editorial frame composition; choose BlockFrame for card-heavy web layouts and maximalist UI.
- Biennale Yellow vs Cartesian: choose Biennale Yellow for literary warmth and atmospheric color; choose Cartesian for architectural restraint and near-monochrome structure.
- Capsule vs BlockFrame: choose Capsule for rounded elegance; choose BlockFrame for square, loud neo-brutalism.

## Hybrid policy

Use a hybrid only when explicitly requested. Select one style as the structural base and borrow at most one named atom from the secondary style. State the borrowed atom and verify it does not violate the base style's explicit Don't rules.
