## Reusable Style Contract

This contract is prescriptive guidance for the independent example, not an official brand standard. Source measurements remain labeled above; token and responsive choices below belong to this adaptation. Reuse the visual system on the user's own content.

Best for: Modular productivity platforms, developer utilities, collaboration suites, and technical product showcases. Avoid when: A dark terminal aesthetic or playful pastel-card identity is needed.

### Signature Atoms

- White lab(100 0 0), near-black lab(2.51107 0.242703 -0.886115), pink rgb(253, 54, 110).
- Manrope with Noto Sans SC; adapted hero 68px, weight 400, line-height 1.12, tracking -.045em.
- Headline gradient oklch(0.845546 0.104311 10.817), oklch(0.512356 0.168274 10.817), then near-black ink.
- 56px navigation; hero dots repeat every 18px; broad low dashboard frame has 28px corners.
- Actions use 8px corners; feature cards 18px; shared-edge metric frame and chart 20px; first feature double-width.
- Impact wash rgba(253, 54, 110, 0.07); delivered closing primary labels #250610; chart tracks 9px high.

### Do

- Use open-source Manrope and Noto Sans SC; treat Aeonik Pro only as a visual reference.
- Center light large display over a fine dotted canvas and a broad low white dashboard abstraction.
- Use rose-to-black emphasis in the title and reserve saturated pink for precise action and signal accents.
- Keep metrics shared-edge and make the first service cell wider without turning every tile pink.
- Preserve dark closing-action labels on pink and verify foreground contrast in the headline gradient and pale sections.

### Don't

- Do not turn the measured pink accent into a pastel-card theme across the page.
- Do not use extra-heavy display type or replace the white canvas with a dark terminal scene.
- Do not add copied bandwidth numbers, code, or customer data inside the decorative dashboard.
- Do not use low-contrast white closing-action labels on the pink fill.
- Do not copy Appwrite logos, trademarks, screenshots, illustrations, or marketing copy.
